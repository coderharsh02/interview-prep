## 🎥 **Miles IT – Striven Software Engineer Role**

### 📝 Pre-recorded Video Interview Questions (Approx. 30 minutes, 10 questions)

---

### **1. Tell us about yourself.**

“Can you share about yourself? Tell me a little bit about you in general.
What did you do in high school?
Did you like those years?
What did you do after high school—did you go straight to work or college?
Did you enjoy those years?”

---

### **2. Why IT and why software development?**

“What made you choose this field?
Tell me a bit about your journey and how you reached the point of applying here today.
Why do you think this is the right career path for you?”

### **4. Tell us about your most recent project.**

“What was your role and key contributions?
